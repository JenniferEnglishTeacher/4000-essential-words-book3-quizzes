Part 1: Word Bank

Get familiar with all 20 words before you test yourself. Each row shows the word, its part of speech, an example sentence with the word in bold, and a "Listen" button to hear the sentence read aloud.

Part 2: Fill-in-the-Blank Practice

Read each passage carefully. Every blank shows the word's first letter plus one box per missing letter - use the context clues in the sentence to figure out the word, then type the rest of the letters. Click Check Answers when a passage is complete. Correct words turn green; incorrect ones turn red and clear so you can try again. If a word is still wrong on your second try, the correct spelling will be revealed in amber so you're not stuck - just make a mental note to review that word.
